# Code of Conduct

Serpentine follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on
the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@avijitbhuin21](https://github.com/avijitbhuin21)). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
participants, in line with the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
